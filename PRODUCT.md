# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary Audience**: High-end creative commissioners, including creative directors at independent and major record labels, publishing houses, cultural editors, and contemporary art curators/collectors seeking bespoke artistic commissions, high-impact album cover concepts, typographic editorial layouts, and contemporary sacred art.
- **Secondary Audience**: Creative agencies, authors, musicians, and brands seeking distinctive visual identity, book/fanzine design, or conceptual visual direction that transcends generic digital minimalism.

## Product Purpose

- Serve as the official interactive digital monograph, visual archive, and commercial commission portal for multidisciplinary visual artist and graphic designer Wilmar Machado.
- Showcase an elite curated collection of 10 signature works (2024–2026) across contemporary sacred art, editorial design, conceptual photography, and graphic art.
- Transform the visitor experience from a conventional static portfolio into an immersive, museum-grade virtual exhibition, facilitating immediate dialogue and high-value project inquiries.

## Positioning

- A distinctive creative threshold positioned where sacred mysticism, Swiss editorial rigor, and chiaroscuro tension converge: *"The visual architecture of memory and the sculptural tension of form."*
- Unlike conventional agency portfolios built on repetitive minimalist grids or generic templates, this archive places the viewer directly inside a 3D Fibonacci mathematical sphere and a cinematic 30FPS parallax prologue, backed by deep curatorial and technical documentation for every individual piece.

## Operating Context

- Web browser environments across desktop displays and mobile touchscreens.
- High-resolution screens where fine-art textures, paper grain (Hahnemühle 308g, 310g fine art, cotton paper), and bespoke typography can be inspected at 220% macro zoom.
- Evaluated by discerning clients and creative leads making commission decisions under tight project schedules; requires instant switching between 3D exploratory immersion and structured 2D catalog scanning.
- Timezone & geography: Studio rooted in Valledupar, Cesar, Colombia (UTC-5), operating with global international remote reach and in-person sessions by appointment.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Signature Hero Experiences**: Full-bleed cinematic video prologue (`video.mp4`) with smooth wheel/touch iris zoom parallax transition into the interactive 3D Fibonacci sphere.
- **Dual Viewing Modes**: Fluid toggle between the 3D Fibonacci spatial sphere (orbit physics, inertial drag, auto-rotation toggle, spatial zoom) and the flat Catalog Grid view (10 works index).
- **Deep Curatorial Inspection**: Modal lightbox featuring technical specifications (Creative Role, Medium/Support, Bespoke Typography, Client/Publisher, Year) and a tri-modal visual renderer (Obra Final, Boceto & Proceso monochrome/grid analysis, Macro Textura 220% detail inspection).
- **Direct Commission Routing**: Pre-populated project proposal drawer and direct WhatsApp integration (`+57 316 329 6958`) linked directly from each artwork.
- **Curatorial Discipline Filtering**: Horizontal scroll-protected filter bar for instant hardware-accelerated classification (Arte Sacro, Diseño Editorial, Fotografía, Arte Gráfico).
- **Autonomous Bilingual System**: 100% client-side, zero-dependency offline translation engine dynamically converting UI, curatorial notes, technical specifications, and metadata between Spanish (`ES`) and English (`EN`).
- **Spotlight Search & Focus Mode**: Fast curatorial search modal (Ctrl+K) and distraction-free Focus Exhibition mode.
- **Studio Control Console**: Master administrative console (`wilmar2026`) for live text, project, style, and contact management with JSON export/backup.
- **IP Protection Constraints**: Strict prohibition of direct asset downloads; removed external Google Drive download buttons, protected media guards, disabled dragging and context menus on master image/video assets.
- **Technical Constraints**: Zero build steps or bundler dependencies; built purely with semantic HTML5, Vanilla CSS3 tokens, and ES6+ modular JavaScript. Runs directly via static file serving (`python3 -m http.server 8000` or direct file open).

## Product Principles

- **Artifact First**: The interface recedes to let the artwork, texture, and visual narrative dominate every viewport without interface clutter.
- **Absolute Autonomy**: The portfolio functions flawlessly offline without external API dependencies, CDNs, or compilation steps.
- **Curatorial Rigor**: Every project is presented with museum-grade precision, documenting creative roles, paper stocks, print mediums, and bespoke typography.
- **Frictionless Conversion**: From any point in the 3D sphere or lightbox, a commissioner can transition in one click to a pre-filled proposal or direct WhatsApp dialogue.
- **Respect for Intellectual Property**: The artist's work is presented with full visual fidelity while strictly safeguarding original high-resolution master assets against unauthorized downloading.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Full keyboard navigation support (Esc to close modals/search, arrows for lightbox artwork navigation, Ctrl+K for search).
- Semantic HTML5 structure with ARIA attributes (`role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, `aria-label`, `aria-live="polite"`).
- High-contrast visual hierarchies across dark aesthetic surfaces.
- Reduced motion and audio muted by default respecting user sensory preferences.
